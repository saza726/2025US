機場入境 (Immigration)

待多久? "I will be staying for 7 days."
有東西申報嗎? "I have nothing to declare."
交通 & 問路 (Transport)
💡 加油技巧: 若刷卡機問 ZIP Code，試試 "00000"。
加油 (加滿/普通油) "Fill it up with Regular, please."
Uber 確認 "Is this the ride for [Your Name]?"
請問洗手間在哪? "Excuse me, where is the restroom?"
請問這個地址怎麼走? "How do I get to this address?"
餐廳點餐 (Dining)
兩位用餐 "Table for two, please."
請給我菜單 "Can I see the menu, please?"
我要外帶 "To go, please." (or "Take out")
買單/結帳 "Can we have the check, please?"
購物 & 買票 (Shopping)
我要買一張成人票 "One adult ticket, please."
可以試穿嗎? "Can I try this on?"
有別的尺寸/顏色嗎? "Do you have this in a larger size / other colors?"
請給我收據 "Can I have a receipt?"
住宿 & 求助 (Hotel/Help)
辦理入住 "Check-in, please. My name is..."
可以寄放行李嗎? "Can I leave my luggage here?"
